Cobblemon Ranger

Cobblemon Ranger brings the Capture Styler from Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs to Cobblemon. Instead of throwing a Poké Ball, right-click a wild Pokémon with your styler and draw loops around it until it trusts you. Your new friend fights by your side, helps you get past obstacles, and then heads back home.

Requires Cobblemon (1.6.1 or newer) and Kotlin for Forge on NeoForge 1.21.1. Install it on both the client and the server.

🎯 The capture minigame

Right-click a wild Pokémon with a styler to open the capture screen. Hold left click and draw loops around the Pokémon to fill its friendship gauge. If the Pokémon, or one of its attacks, touches your line, the line breaks and your styler loses energy.

  • Attacks depend on the Pokémon's type: charges, projectile bursts, shockwaves and eruptions.
  • Rarer, higher-level Pokémon move faster, attack more often and need more loops.
  • The arena matches where the Pokémon is standing: meadows, forests, beaches, snow, caves, the Nether and more.

🤝 Friend Pokémon

A captured Pokémon joins your party as a temporary friend so it can battle with you. It returns to the wild by itself once your next battle is over. You can also send friends home at any time by sneaking and using the styler in the air, or with /ranger release.

✨ Poké Assists

During a capture, your friends and your Partner Pokémon appear in a bar under the arena. Click one, or press its number, to call for help. Every type has its own effect:

Type Effect
Normal The capture line gets 50% longer
Fire A ring of flames traps the target
Water Hold still to blow a bubble, flick to launch it. Bigger bubbles trap the target for longer
Grass Tall grass sprouts and slows the target to a crawl
Electric Recharges your styler
Ice Your strokes become ice walls. Box the target in to freeze it
Ground Your next loop becomes quicksand the target can't leave
Rock Your next stroke becomes a boulder wall the target can't cross
Flying Blows attacks away and pins the target in a whirlwind
Bug A sticky web holds the target whenever it touches it
Psychic The target and its attacks slow down
Ghost The target runs to a corner and cowers
Dark The target can't see your line, so its attacks miss
Poison The friendship gauge fills by itself
Fairy Your next loops count double
Dragon A big burst of friendship
Steel Your line can't be broken for a while
Fighting Blocks the next hit and stuns the target

Type matchups matter: an assist the target is weak to works better, one it resists works less, and one it's immune to does nothing. A friend goes back to the wild after it assists.

⭐ Partner Pokémon

Send out one of your own Pokémon and use the styler on it to make it your Partner Pokémon. While it's in your party it follows you around, helps with field moves without leaving, and gains friendship from your captures. Its own Poké Assist is 50% stronger than a friend's, but you charge it first: every loop you draw around a wild Pokémon fills its meter, even across captures, and it's ready at 10 loops.

🌲 Field Moves

Use the styler on an obstacle and a friend or your partner clears it for you:

  • Cut fells a whole tree or clears leaves, vines and cobwebs (Grass, Bug, Steel or Dark types).
  • Rock Smash breaks a 3×3×3 chunk of stone, cobblestone, sandstone or gravel (Fighting, Rock, Ground or Steel).
  • Soak puts out fires and cools lava into obsidian (Water or Ice).
  • Surf lets you ride across water for two minutes (Water).

Pokémon that know the move itself can help too. Block protection from other mods is respected.

📜 Ranger HQ, missions and ranks

Missions come from Rangers: villagers who work at a Ranger HQ Terminal. Craft one, or find a Ranger HQ house in plains, desert, savanna, snowy and taiga villages. Use a styler on a Ranger to be offered a mission: capture Pokémon (sometimes of a certain type), clear obstacles with field moves, use Poké Assists, battle alongside friends, or bring supplies. Report back for emeralds, styler XP, Ranger points and sometimes an upgrade module. You can take one mission per Minecraft day.

Every mission you complete levels the Ranger up from Novice to Master, unlocking harder missions with bigger rewards and new trades. Your Ranger rank caps how hard a mission you can take, so there's no skipping ahead.

Ranger points raise your Ranger rank from 1 to 10. Your rank and current mission show at the top left while you hold a styler.

🌀 Ranger Signs

Press G with a styler in your inventory and draw a sign in one stroke to call a legendary Pokémon for help. New signs unlock as your rank goes up:

Sign Shape Rank Effect
Celebi Circle 1 Makes nearby plants grow
Suicune Wave 2 Heals you and your party
Entei Triangle 3 Cooks and smelts dropped items nearby
Raikou Lightning bolt 4 Wild Pokémon nearby glow, and shinies are named
Latios V 5 Flies you home
Groudon M 6 Clears the weather
Kyogre Spiral 7 Brings rain
Cresselia Crescent 8 Ends the night
Darkrai Star 9 Brings the night
Rayquaza S, drawn upwards 10 Launches you into the sky

Each sign costs 10 styler energy and has its own cooldown. The weather and time signs also share a world-wide cooldown, so players on a server can't keep flipping the weather.

🎨 Stylers, energy and upgrades

  • 16 colours. Craft a styler from a glass pane, two iron ingots, redstone and a dye (pane on top, iron either side of the redstone, dye underneath). Combine a styler with a dye to recolour it without losing upgrades.
  • Styler energy is shown as durability. It recharges slowly while you carry the styler and can be topped up with redstone at an anvil. Stylers never break.
  • Styler levels. Captures and missions give your styler XP. Each level, up to Lv. 20, adds energy, line length and loop power.
  • Upgrade modules (Energy, Line Length, Loop Power) are found in structure chests: dungeons, mineshafts, temples, strongholds, ancient cities, shipwrecks, trial chambers and more. Install one at a Ranger HQ Terminal with a redstone block, string or amethyst shard. The terminal also charges your styler to full every 10 minutes and shows your current mission.

⌨️ Commands

Command What it does
/ranger friends Lists your friend Pokémon
/ranger release Sends your friends home
/ranger partner Shows your Partner Pokémon (clear removes it)
/ranger mission Shows your mission (abandon drops it)
/ranger rank Shows your rank and Ranger Signs
